Meet Kaz & Meli

Mum Kaz, dog mum Meli

Three Wishes Gifts has been a dream for many years…

We're a mum-and-daughter team building a business. Mum Kaz (mum of 3, hence the Three Wishes) and dog mum Meli. Between us we have years of experience building operations systems and brands for some seriously impressive companies.

In our professional lives, we've helped businesses get organised, look beautiful, and run smoothly. We're the people behind the systems, the strategies, and the polished brands that make everything feel effortless from the outside.

But at home? We were just as overwhelmed as everyone else.

We needed organisation that actually fit our lives — not another complicated system that added stress instead of removing it.

We wanted to feel on top of things and enjoy the ride, without the guilt, the pressure, or the constant sense of falling behind.

So we built it ourselves. Clean, simple, beautifully designed digital and print tools that help real women manage their days with a little more ease and a lot more joy.

And gifts for friends and family throughout the stages of their lives, so we celebrate the special moments.

That's the heart of Three Wishes — because every woman deserves to feel good about herself, her space, and her day, and those dear to her. We want our products to be that small thing that makes everything feel a little more magical.

We hope they do exactly that for you.

— Kaz & Meli ✨
